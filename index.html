OUR PASSION & HERITAGE

Where Passion Meets the Perfect Cup

Founded in 2014, Aroma & Bean started with a simple vision: to elevate the everyday coffee ritual into a sensory experience. We travel worldwide to connect with passionate coffee growers who share our commitment to fair wages, soil health, and immaculate harvesting practices.

Every batch is roasted in our custom vintage cast-iron roaster, continuously monitored for temperature and caramelization to unlock complex floral, fruity, and chocolate notes inherent to each bean origin.
